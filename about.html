ABOUT US

We connect the ride, not just the road.

RideConnect is being built as a two-sided mobility marketplace for customers and transport partners.

OUR IDEA

Make local mobility easier to discover.

Customers often need a reliable way to find a suitable car and driver. Vehicle owners and drivers need more opportunities to find genuine trip demand. RideConnect is designed to bring both sides together.

Our long-term goal is to create a transparent marketplace where customers can request trips and partners can accept suitable opportunities.

Customer↓RideConnect Marketplace↓Driver / Vehicle Partner

Commission can be charged on eligible completed bookings.

OUR PRINCIPLES

Simple, transparent, partner-friendly.

Customer first

Clear trip information and a simple booking journey.

Partner growth

Help drivers and owners discover more demand.

Trust & transparency

Build clear processes around bookings, pricing and commissions.
